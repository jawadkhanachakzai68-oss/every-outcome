KNOWN GAPS
- Results are for the standard Catanatron bot, not human players, and domestic trading is unused (see Limits chapter).
- "Variety first" is a rule I wrote; it is not proven optimal. Only five rules were tested; variety first (24.7%) matches the 25% fair share, interval 23.3-26.1; it does not beat the standard players.
- Rule win rates carry about +/- 2 points (1,500 games each; +/- 1.4 for the two rules run on 3,500). Replication on fresh seeds gave the same ordering (most dots 17.4%, variety first 25.7%).
- The 22%-vs-fair comparison in the 2025 paper is for three players (fair = 33%); the script says so.
- The million-game study measures opening quality, not wins; the script says "opening".
